# VAYAS reading edition

The full HTML reading page is `public/paper-vayas.html`, served at `/research/vayas-age-stratified-asr`.

- **Publication:** Shubham Kumar Gupta and Sania Gurung, *VAYAS: An Age-Stratified Zero-Shot Audit of Hindi Speech Recognition*, Zenodo preprint v1, 30 August 2026.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22176362
- **Paper license:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as recorded on the Zenodo deposit. This attribution applies to the paper and its published figures, not a license claim for the code repository.
- **Source:** [LaTeX manuscript](https://github.com/kshubham090/vayas-kosh/blob/1a3301e2158c04fc60df3fc8a0d204eaf41e7820/Research/Paper%201/vayas/docs/paper/main.tex) and its bibliography at source revision `1a3301e2158c04fc60df3fc8a0d204eaf41e7820`.
- **PDF:** `/research/vayas-age-stratified-asr.pdf` is an unchanged copy of the Zenodo PDF. SHA-256: `c9701f382a3e8d300453255c3959890c2335c3b919834f3fc4cea97e70a9dedc`.

The HTML edition was prepared on 8 October 2026 by converting the manuscript with Pandoc, then applying the portfolio's paper typography, semantic section structure, linked citations/cross-references, native MathML and accessible table/figure navigation. It retains the full manuscript, five tables, six published figures and eleven references. Web formatting differs from the PDF; results and conclusions are not rewritten. The title footnote is retained. Affiliations and publication date come from Zenodo metadata; author names use their full PDF form.
